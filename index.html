Flock Off

A two-player lane battle for iPhone. Send your herd up five lanes, push the other herd back, and be the first to claim 3 of 5 lanes.

Tap a lane to send your next animal. When two herds meet, they push. Bigger animals push harder, and animals in a line push together. Reach the far end and the lane is yours for good.

Play the computer on Easy, Normal or Hard, play a friend on two iPhones on the same Wi-Fi, or play online with a room code or quick match. Start with sheep and unlock goats, pigs, alpacas, cows and bison as you level up. Herds change the look only.

Flock Off is free for iPhone with iOS 17 or later. It has no ads, no account and no tracking. It sends anonymous usage summaries that you can turn off in Settings. Playing the computer works with no network at all.
